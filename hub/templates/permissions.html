Permission Groups

A group grants a set of capabilities over a set of machines. A person's effective access is the union of every group they belong to.

Addresses in ALLOWED_EMAILS are break-glass superusers: they hold every capability over every machine regardless of the groups below, and they can always sign in. That is what lets you create the first group here, and what stops a mistake on this page from locking everyone out. Everyone else must be in a group to sign in at all.

Machines

Machines are taken from Active Directory, and follow it: a PC moved into one of these OUs joins this group at the next directory sync, and one moved out leaves it. Nested OUs are included.

Members

Directory groups optional

Anyone whose sign-in provider says they are in one of these groups gets this permission group, without being listed above by name. Paste the group's object ID (Entra), distinguished name, or name — whichever your provider sends. Membership is read once, at sign-in: a change in the directory takes effect the next time the person signs in.
